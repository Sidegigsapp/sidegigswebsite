Side gigs you can pick up in Kerala

Most gigs are a few hours or a few days long, so they fit around college, a full-time job or family time.

Event & wedding crew

Set up venues, guide guests and help with catering at weddings, expos and college fests — Kerala's wedding season keeps this work busy.

Promoters & brand activation

Run sampling stalls and promotions in malls and supermarkets, usually on weekends.

Delivery

Deliver food, groceries and parcels on your own two-wheeler, in the hours you choose.

Café, restaurant & kitchen

Evening and lunch-rush shifts as a barista, server or kitchen helper.

Retail & festive sales

Extra hands in shops during Onam, Vishu, Christmas and sale seasons.

Tutoring

Home and online tuition for school students in the evenings.

Surveys & field work

Short field surveys and data collection, often paid by the day.

Work from home

Data entry, social media help, Malayalam voice-over and translation gigs you can do online.

Questions about part-time work in Kerala

How can I find part-time jobs in Kerala?

Download the free Sidegigs app, sign in with your Google account and set your location. You will see part-time jobs and side gigs posted by employers near you in Kerala, and you can apply in the app with no interview round.

Are there part-time jobs for students in Kerala?

Yes. Many gigs on Sidegigs are short shifts in the evening, on weekends or for a single day, such as event crew, promoters, tutoring and café work, which fit around college hours. You need to be at least 18 years old to use Sidegigs.

Do I need a resume or an interview?

There is no interview round for gigs on Sidegigs. Some employers may ask for a resume before they hire, so it helps to keep one ready. You apply from your profile in the app.

Which cities in Kerala does Sidegigs cover?

Sidegigs works anywhere in Kerala, including Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, Kozhikode, Thrissur, Kannur, Kollam, Kottayam, Palakkad, Malappuram and Alappuzha. The gigs you see depend on what employers have posted near your location.

How do I get paid for a gig?

Pay for each gig is shown on the listing and is paid to you directly by the employer who posted it. Sidegigs does not handle payments, so agree the details with the employer before you start.

Is Sidegigs free?

Yes. The Sidegigs app is free to download and use on Android and iPhone.

Can businesses in Kerala post part-time jobs?

Yes. Shops, restaurants, event organisers and other employers can post gigs on the Sidegigs app and hear from people nearby who are ready to work.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

Sidegigs is a mobile application operated by Wrkshift Pvt. Ltd., Kerala, India. The platform connects students and individuals with part-time and gig-based opportunities posted by employers.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect user information when you use the Sidegigs application. Our practices comply with the Google Play Developer User Data policies and applicable laws.

By using the Sidegigs app, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Google Account Information

Sidegigs allows users to sign in using their Google account. When signing in with Google, we may receive basic profile information such as your name and email address from Google for authentication purposes.

This information is used only to create and manage your account on the Sidegigs platform.

b) Job and Platform Usage Information

When using the Sidegigs platform, we may collect information related to your activity within the app, including:

  • Job postings created by employers
  • Job applications submitted by users
  • Job participation status
  • Messages exchanged within the platform
  • Ratings or feedback between users
  • App interaction and usage data

c) Location Information

Location access may be requested to display nearby job opportunities based on the user's area.

Sidegigs does not collect location data in the background and does not continuously track users.

Location access is optional and can be disabled through device settings.

d) Device and Technical Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • IP address
  • Log files and crash reports
  • Diagnostic and performance data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Enable job discovery and job applications
  • Allow communication between students and employers
  • Send important service notifications
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Detect fraud, misuse, or policy violations
  • Comply with legal obligations

Sidegigs does not sell or rent personal user data.

3. Platform Role

Sidegigs operates solely as a technology platform that connects job seekers and employers.

Sidegigs:

  • Does not employ users
  • Does not guarantee employment opportunities
  • Does not manage payment agreements between users

Any agreements, work arrangements, or payments occur directly between employers and workers.

4. Age Restriction (18+ Only)

Sidegigs is intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older.

By creating an account and using the platform, users confirm that they are at least 18 years old. If we become aware that a user under the age of 18 is using the platform, we may suspend or remove the account.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share limited information with:

  • Employers or job applicants when required for job matching
  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Analytics and crash-reporting services
  • Government or legal authorities when required by law

All third-party services are required to comply with applicable data protection standards.

6. Data Retention

User information is retained:

  • While the account remains active
  • For a limited period after account deletion where required by law

After this period, data is either permanently deleted or anonymized.

7. User Rights

Users have the right to:

  • View and edit personal information
  • Request a copy of their data
  • Withdraw consent for optional permissions
  • Permanently delete their account

8. Account Deletion

Users can delete their account at any time from:

Profile → Settings → Delete Account

Once deleted:

  • Account access is removed
  • Personal data is scheduled for deletion according to our retention policy

9. Notifications

Sidegigs may send notifications related to:

  • Job opportunities or updates
  • Job application status
  • Important account-related information

Notification permissions are optional and can be managed through device settings.

10. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Sidegigs may use cookies or similar technologies for:

  • App analytics
  • Performance monitoring
  • Crash diagnostics

These technologies help improve the platform and do not personally identify users.

11. Third-Party Services

Sidegigs may use trusted third-party services for:

  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure
  • Analytics and performance monitoring
  • Crash reporting
  • Notification delivery

Each service provider is required to follow applicable privacy and data protection laws.

12. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect user information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

While we strive to protect personal data, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

13. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect service improvements or legal requirements.

Users will be notified of significant changes through the application or other appropriate communication channels.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2025

Sidegigs Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (“Sidegigs”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, website, and services.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

1.1 Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Government ID details (for KYC)
  • Date of birth, gender, education and skill details
  • Bank account/UPI details (for payouts)
  • Location (to match you with nearby gigs)

1.2 Device and Usage Data

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • App activity, access times, and referring URLs

2.1 How do we get your consent?

By providing your personal information during registration, job applications, or payments, you consent to our collecting and using it for that specific reason only.

For marketing communications, we will ask for your explicit consent, and you can opt out at any time.

4. Payments & KYC

We use Cashfree Payments India for:

  • UPI, credit/debit card, and bank transactions
  • KYC (Know Your Customer) verification

4.1 Compliance with Indian Law

  • All payment data is handled in accordance with RBI, NPCI, and PCI-DSS guidelines.
  • UPI transactions adhere to NPCI mandates — including merchant display names, transaction limits, and local data storage.

6. Security

We take industry-standard precautions, including:

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, no method of transmission over the Internet or mobile platform is 100% secure.

We may use cookies or local storage to:

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though it may affect functionality.

8. Age of Consent

By using Sidegigs, you confirm that you are at least the age of majority in your state, or that you are the guardian providing consent for a minor dependent to use the platform.

9. Data Location & Retention

All user data is securely stored in India in accordance with Indian data localization laws.

We retain personal data only as long as needed for:

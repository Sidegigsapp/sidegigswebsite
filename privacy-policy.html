Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

SIDEGIGS ("we", "our", "us") operates a mobile application that connects students with part-time and gig-based job opportunities posted by employers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect user data in compliance with Google Play Developer User Data policies and applicable laws.

By using the SIDEGIGS app, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Date of birth (for age verification – 18+)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Education or skill details (students)
  • Company details (employers)

b) Job & Usage Information

  • Job postings and applications
  • Job participation status
  • Ratings and feedback
  • In-app messages between users
  • App interaction data

c) Location Information

  • Approximate location (city/area) to show nearby jobs
  • Precise location only if required for job attendance and only with user permission

d) Device & Technical Information

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • IP address
  • Log files, crash reports, and diagnostics

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Match students with relevant job opportunities
  • Enable communication between students and employers
  • Send service-related notifications
  • Improve app performance and security
  • Prevent fraud and misuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell or rent user data.

3. Age Restriction (18+ Only)

SIDEGIGS is strictly intended for users 18 years of age or older.

We collect date of birth during signup for age verification. If an account is found to belong to a user under 18, it will be suspended and deleted.

4. Location Data Usage

Location access is used only to:

  • Display nearby job opportunities
  • Improve job discovery accuracy

Location data:

  • Is collected only with user consent
  • Is not tracked in the background
  • Can be disabled anytime via device settings

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We may share limited user data with:

  • Employers (job-related information only)
  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Analytics and crash-reporting services
  • Government or legal authorities when required by law

All third-party services are required to follow strict data protection standards.

6. Data Retention

We retain user data:

  • While the account remains active
  • Up to 24 months after account deletion, only where legally required

After this period, data is permanently deleted or anonymized.

7. User Rights

Users have the right to:

  • View and edit personal information
  • Request a copy of their data
  • Withdraw consent for optional permissions
  • Permanently delete their account

These options are available within the app or by contacting support.

8. Account Deletion

Users can delete their account at any time from:

Profile → Settings → Delete Account

Once deleted:

  • Account access is removed
  • Personal data is scheduled for deletion according to our retention policy

9. Notifications

SIDEGIGS may send notifications related to:

  • Job updates
  • Application status
  • Important account-related information

Notification permissions are optional and can be managed through device settings.

10. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies or similar technologies for:

  • App analytics
  • Performance monitoring
  • Crash diagnostics

These technologies do not personally identify users.

11. Third-Party Services

SIDEGIGS uses trusted third-party services for:

  • Cloud hosting
  • Analytics
  • Crash reporting
  • Notification delivery

Each service provider complies with applicable privacy and data protection laws.

12. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Users will be notified of significant changes through the app or other appropriate means.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

Compliance with Indian Laws: This Privacy Policy is compliant with the Information Technology Act, 2000 (IT Act) and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

1. Information We Collect

In accordance with Indian data protection laws, we collect the following categories of information:

Personal Information:

  • Full name, email address, and mobile number
  • Date of birth and gender
  • PAN (Permanent Account Number) for tax purposes
  • Aadhaar number (optional, for identity verification)
  • Bank account details for payment processing

Professional Information:

  • Educational qualifications and certificates
  • Work experience and skills
  • Professional references
  • Work preferences and availability

Location and Device Information:

  • Current location for job matching
  • Device information and IP address
  • Usage patterns and preferences

2. Legal Basis for Data Collection

We collect and process your personal data based on the following legal grounds under Indian law:

  • Consent: When you explicitly agree to our data processing
  • Contract Performance: To provide our part-time job matching services
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with Indian tax and employment laws
  • Legitimate Interest: To improve our services and prevent fraud

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing your job seeker profile
  • Matching you with relevant part-time job opportunities
  • Processing payments and generating invoices
  • Compliance with Indian tax regulations (TDS, GST)
  • Background verification and skill assessment
  • Communication regarding job opportunities and platform updates
  • Fraud prevention and security measures

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • With Employers: To facilitate job matching and placement
  • With Government Authorities: As required by Indian laws (tax, employment, etc.)
  • With Service Providers: For payment processing, verification, and technical support
  • Legal Requirements: When required by court orders or legal proceedings

5. Data Security and Retention

We implement security measures as per Indian IT Act requirements:

  • Encryption of sensitive personal data
  • Secure data transmission protocols
  • Regular security audits and assessments
  • Access controls and authentication measures

Data Retention: We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with Indian legal requirements, typically up to 7 years for tax and employment records.

6. Your Rights Under Indian Law

As per Indian data protection laws, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Right to Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to Object: Object to certain types of data processing
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing

7. Compliance with Indian Regulations

We comply with the following Indian laws and regulations:

  • Information Technology Act, 2000
  • Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011
  • Income Tax Act, 1961 (for TDS and tax compliance)
  • GST Act, 2017 (for tax invoice requirements)
  • Shops and Establishments Act (state-specific)
